Day 01

Overnight departure from Delhi / Chandigarh

  1. Meet our representative at the boarding point.
  2. Board an evening AC tempo traveller / bus from Delhi or Chandigarh.
  3. Meet your fellow travellers. The trip captain helps with seating and luggage.
  4. Once everyone is settled, the vehicle departs for an overnight journey.
Day 02

McLeodganj sightseeing and hotel stay

  1. Reach Dharamshala / McLeodganj in the morning and check in to the hotel.
  2. Rest, freshen up, then head out for local sightseeing.
  3. Visit Bhagsunag Temple, Bhagsunag Waterfall, Shiva Café, Dalai Lama Temple, St. John Church and Mall Road.
  4. Evening local market. Dinner with bonfire and light music. Overnight at the hotel.

Days three and four

Dharamshala views, then the road home.

Stadium, war memorial and tea garden — then an early departure so you reach Delhi / Chandigarh by morning.

Day 03

Dharamshala sightseeing and hotel stay

  1. Wake up early and have breakfast at the hotel.
  2. Cab for Dharamshala sightseeing: Cricket Stadium, War Memorial and Tea Garden.
  3. Head back to the hotel for leisure time.
  4. Dinner and overnight stay at the hotel.
Day 04

Departure to Delhi / Chandigarh

  1. Wake up early and have breakfast.
  2. Check out and board the vehicle for the return journey.
  3. Reach Delhi / Chandigarh by morning with mountain memories packed in.
