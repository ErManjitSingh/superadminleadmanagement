Day 01

Overnight departure from Delhi / Chandigarh

  1. Meet our representative at the boarding point.
  2. Board an evening AC tempo traveller / bus from Delhi or Chandigarh.
  3. Meet your fellow travellers. The trip captain helps with seating and luggage.
  4. Once everyone is settled, the vehicle departs for an overnight journey.
Day 02

McLeodganj Triund trek and camping

  1. Reach Bhagsu Nag in the morning and have breakfast (own cost).
  2. Begin the hike to Triund. Pit stop at Shepherd Cottage for about 30 minutes.
  3. Reach Triund by noon and enjoy views of the Dhauladhar ranges.
  4. Dinner and overnight stay at the campsite.

Day three

Ridge sunrise, Dharamshala, then the road home.

Descend to Bhagsu Nag, refresh, and cover local sightseeing before the evening departure to Delhi / Chandigarh.

Day 03

Triund return, Dharamshala & departure

  1. Wake up early, have breakfast, and trek back to Bhagsu Nag, Dharamshala.
  2. Pit stop at Shiva Café on the way and relax for about 30 minutes.
  3. Return to the office and refresh.
  4. Cab for local sightseeing, then head back to Delhi / Chandigarh by evening.
