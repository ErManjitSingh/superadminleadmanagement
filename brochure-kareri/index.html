Day 01

Kareri Lake trek & camping

  1. Wake up early and have breakfast.
  2. Take a cab to Kareri village. The walk to Kareri Lake starts from here.
  3. Pit stop at Reoti on the way and unwind for about 30 minutes.
  4. Reach the lake, settle in, and take in the Dhauladhar views.
  5. Dinner and overnight stay at the campsite.
Day 02

Return trek & Dharamshala

  1. Wake up, have breakfast, and start the descent towards Dharamshala.
  2. Pit stop at Kareri village on the way for about 30 minutes.
  3. Evening visit to the local market.
  4. Head home by evening with alpine-lake memories.
