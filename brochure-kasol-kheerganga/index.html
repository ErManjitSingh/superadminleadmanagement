Day 00

Overnight departure from Delhi / Chandigarh

  1. Meet our representative at the boarding point in Delhi or Chandigarh.
  2. Board an evening AC tempo traveller for Kasol.
  3. Meet fellow travellers. The trip captain helps with seating and luggage.
  4. Halt for dinner on the way (own cost), then continue overnight.
Day 01

Kasol sightseeing & Swiss camp

  1. Reach Kasol village in the morning and check in. Rest after the overnight journey.
  2. By noon, local sightseeing: Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara, hot springs, Parvati Valley and Mall Road.
  3. Valley views and a riverside walk.
  4. Bonfire with light music, dinner, and overnight stay at the Swiss camp.

Days two and three

Trek to the hot springs. Camp under the peaks.

Start from Barshaini village. 14 km trail via the 360° viewpoint, camp at Kheerganga, soak in the natural hot water, then ride home.

Day 02

Kheerganga trek & campsite stay

  1. Wake up early, enjoy sunrise, and have breakfast.
  2. Start the trek from Barshaini village — 14 km, via the 360° viewpoint (subject to weather).
  3. Pit stop mid-way and unwind for about 30 minutes.
  4. Reach Kheerganga base camp by noon. Dinner and overnight stay at the campsite.
Day 03

Hot water, return trek & departure

  1. Wake up early, have breakfast, then walk to Kheerganga top for hot water and mountain views.
  2. Pit stop mid-way on the descent to Barshaini for about 30 minutes.
  3. Reach Barshaini, refresh, and restore your energy.
  4. Head back to Delhi / Chandigarh by evening. Reach next morning.
