Day 00

Overnight departure from Delhi

  1. Leave from Delhi around 8:00 PM.
  2. Meet the trip coordinator at the boarding point and settle into the vehicle.
  3. Take a pit stop for dinner on the way.
  4. Continue overnight towards Aut / Jibhi.
Day 01

Jibhi arrival, waterfall & Mini Thailand

  1. Reach Aut in the morning. Private transfer from Aut to Jibhi.
  2. Check in to the cottage, rest, then hike to Jibhi waterfall — quiet, green and less crowded.
  3. Short hike to Mini Thailand, a natural swimming pool in the forest.
  4. Back to the cottage by evening. Dinner and overnight stay.

Days two and three

Jalori views, Tirthan river, then the road home.

Two day-hikes from Jalori Pass — Raghupur Fort for 360° Himalayan views, or Serolsar Lake, especially beautiful when frozen in winter.

Day 02

Jalori Pass · Serolsar Lake / Raghupur Fort

  1. After breakfast, drive to Jalori Pass by private vehicles.
  2. Choose a day hike: Raghupur Fort (preferred — 360° Himalayan views) or Serolsar Lake.
  3. Evening back to Jibhi. Leisure time at the cottage.
  4. Dinner and overnight stay in Jibhi.
Day 03

Chhoie Waterfall, Tirthan River & departure

  1. Wake up, have breakfast, check out, and head to Tirthan Valley — Chhoie Waterfall.
  2. Time by the Tirthan River and cafes for lunch.
  3. Drive to Aut tunnel and board the AC bus for Delhi.
  4. Reach Delhi the next morning.
