Day 01

McLeodganj · Triund trek & camping

  1. Reach Bhagsu Nag and have breakfast (own cost).
  2. Begin the hike to Triund. Pit stop at Shepherd Cottage and unwind for about 30 minutes.
  3. Reach Triund (Karthani) by noon and take in the Dhauladhar views.
  4. Dinner and overnight stay at the campsite.
Day 02

Return trek & Bhagsu Nag departure

  1. Wake up early, have breakfast, and start the descent to Bhagsu Nag.
  2. Pit stop at Shiva Café on the way and relax for about 30 minutes.
  3. Return to the office, refresh, and wrap up the trek.
  4. Head home by noon with Dhauladhar memories packed in.
